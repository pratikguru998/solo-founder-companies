        What's included. Companies whose product is software or runs on it — apps, platforms, marketplaces, online services — started by one person, as widely reported, and grouped by the sector they serve. Where a partner, investor or family member was involved early on, the company's card says so.

        How it's ranked. By the best available size figure: market cap for listed companies, the last private valuation, the price paid in an acquisition, or annual revenue where none of those exist. These measure different things, so treat the ranking as rough orientation. Figures are approximate, rounded and in US dollars; each card shows the measure and the year. Not investment advice.
